Independent inquiry · Range-wide perspective · Freely shared

What do wild steelhead need to persist?

Wild Steelhead Futures brings a broad view to that narrow question, examining how conservation efforts align with the biological threats facing wild steelhead across their entire range, from mountain headwaters to the far reaches of the North Pacific.

Through initial projects and community feedback, I am testing whether an independent, range-wide perspective across institutions, jurisdictions, and environments can help identify gaps and inform conservation decisions.

Research, analysis, and strategic synthesis.
