# Disclosures

Wild Steelhead Futures asks others to support their work with public documents. It should meet the same standard. This page lists my funding, affiliations, and anything that could reasonably be seen as a conflict of interest. I will keep it current.

## What WSF Is

Wild Steelhead Futures is a personal project. It is not a legal entity or a nonprofit organization, and it does not currently solicit donations. I carry out the work myself; no one else is involved at this stage.

## Funding

I cover all of WSF's costs myself. WSF receives no grants, donations, contract funding, or in-kind support.

If that changes, sources of funding will be listed here.
